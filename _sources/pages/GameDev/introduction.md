# Introduction

<p>In my spare time, I have been developing my own game using Unreal Engine 5.5, Blender, and Quixel Mixer. Although still in its early stages, this project has allowed me to acquire a variety of skills, from 3D modelling and texturing to game mechanics and lighting design. I've gained a deeper understanding of the complexities involved in game development, including optimisation, level design, and player experience. This section is organised into several tabs that showcase different aspects of my progress, from the game’s current state to the various challenges and creative solutions I’ve encountered along the way.</p> 

<p> The ultimate goal of this project is to create a game set in a sci-fi dystopian future, where the main character stumbles upon a previously-thought lost cruise ship. According to company policy, when encountering lost ships, it is mandatory to retrieve the black box flight data and record its location. Onboard, the player must uncover the mysterious events that led to the ship’s fate and the disappearance of its crew. Drawing inspiration from the AI principles in *Alien: Isolation*, I aim to create an adaptive AI system that responds to the player's behaviours, with no pre-programmed routes or objectives. The game will combine the eerie atmosphere of *Alien: Isolation* with the horrifying elements of *Dead Space*, offering an immersive, suspenseful narrative that gradually reveals the terrifying story of what really happened aboard the ship. </p>
